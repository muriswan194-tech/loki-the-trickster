# Third-party art assets

These small source assets are stored locally in the Unity project. The game does not fetch anything at runtime.

| Asset | Use | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marble 01, 2K JPG diffuse, normal and roughness maps | Cream Asgard courtyard flagstones | [Poly Haven asset page](https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01) | CC0 |
| Gothic Statue, 1K FBX and diffuse | Recolored as an Asgard sentinel landmark | [Poly Haven asset page](https://polyhaven.com/a/gothic_statue) | CC0 |
| Tiles 131, 1K JPG maps | Alternate golden tile set retained for iteration; not used by the current scene | [ambientCG asset page](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Tiles131) | CC0 1.0 Universal |

Poly Haven states these assets are CC0, so they can be used, modified and included in a game. The scene uses the statue as decorative scenery, not as a copyrighted character or franchise asset.
